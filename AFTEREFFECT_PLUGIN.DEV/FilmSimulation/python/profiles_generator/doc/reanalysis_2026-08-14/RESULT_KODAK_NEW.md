## 10. KODAK DATA BOOK.pdf (1495 pp)
- Identity/era: "Kodak Data Books of Applied Photography", KODAK LIMITED, London (UK; priced in guineas). Loose-leaf, sheets individually dated; issue years span ~1948–1968; update-sheet imprint "PDDB-61/11-68" (p20) and Ektachrome EF 7241/7242 (1966+) present ⇒ this copy as-bound reflects LATE 1950s–1968 state, digitized 2021 (p1). NOT a pure 1952 snapshot: monochrome speeds are post-1960 ASA (no safety factor).
- Structure: Vol 1–5 map p1; section list p594/876/1140. Volume 5 = FILMS (section FM): contents pp1150–1151; data sheets pp1170–1495.
- Speed/classification master tables: pp33–35 (monochrome + colour meter settings), pp896–900 (Vol 4 SE-1 classification incl. safelights/contrast class).
- Films documented (start pages): Ektachrome D/B E-3 1170; High-Speed Ektachrome 1180/1196; Ektachrome EF 7241/7242 1188; Kodachrome (old) 1218; Kodachrome II 1224; Kodachrome-X 1232; Ektacolor Prof S/L 1238; Ektacolor Print 1244; Kodacolor 1252; Kodacolor-X 1256; 16mm Tri-X Neg Movie 1262 (ASA 320D/250T); 16mm Plus-X Neg Movie 1266 (80D/64T); High-Contrast Pan Movie 1274; Tri-X Reversal 1278 (200/160); Plus-X Reversal 1282 (50/40); Kodalith 1312/1320/1378/1382; Process Sheet 1328; Commercial Ortho 1332 (ASA 50, D-61a gammas); Ortho-Royal 1342 (400); Plus-X Sheet 1346 & Plus-X Pan Sheet 1352 (ASA 125, D-76 gamma 0.55/0.7); Panchro-Royal 1364 (400, "double Super-XX speed"); Commercial Fine-Grain 1370; Separation-Neg Type 1&2 1386/1390; Royal-X Pan Sheet 1394 (1250, DK-50); Tri-X Pan Roll 1400 (400, gamma 0.55/0.7 D-76); Tri-X Pan Prof 1406; Panatomic-X Roll 1412 (40, gamma 0.65/0.8); Plus-X Pan Prof 1418; Verichrome Pan 1426 (125, gamma 0.65/0.8); Royal-X Pan Roll 1432; Panatomic-X Mini 1438 (32); Plus-X Pan Mini 1448 (125); Quick-Finish 1458 (50); 35mm Fine-Grain Positive 1462 (blue-sensitive, "high resolving power", 0.0055-in clear base, unbacked); Super-XX Aero 1464 (200, unbacked base); Recording R.55/R.60 1468 (gamma 1.5/1.0); Microdak 1472; Panatomic-X Aero 1476; Plus-X Aerographic 1480; Kodagraph 1484–1495.
- Super-XX Sheet: ASA 200 in tables p33/p899 (FM-37); its own FM sheet was replaced in later issues by Tri-X Pan Professional (contents p1151); Ortho-X appears in early index (p5,143) but has no surviving FM sheet here.
- Common physical spec of camera films: "safety base carrying an anti-halation backing which clears during development" (e.g., 1352, 1400, 1426); miniature films: "safety base tinted for the prevention of halation" (1438, 1448) — base-tint AH, not remjet.
- Granularity/resolving theory: SE section pp892–917 (graininess, resolving power, spectral-sens definition, 15 s standard exposure).
