## 1. E2 — the row's headline warning pointed the wrong way

E2's trap, verbatim: *"the sheets plot the equivalent energy needed … so log sensitivity is its
NEGATION — the peak of the curve is its LOWEST drawn point"*, with the caution that a wrong sign
yields a mirrored set passing every band and ordering check.

⚠ **The caution is right and the diagnosis is backwards. Negating them is what would have produced
the mirror.** The sheets' prose describes the MEASUREMENT; Polaroid plots its reciprocal.

Four independent proofs, the last two decisive:

1. The 667 edition captions its axis **"Spectral Sensitivity (cm²/erg)"** — area per unit energy.
   Energy required would be erg/cm². The sheet states the direction itself.
2. Every curve falls steeply at its long-wave end, which is a sensitising dye running out. Inverted,
   each film would reach **peak** sensitivity exactly where its data stops.
3. **Peak plotted value rises with film speed across all four sheets** — EI 50 → 9.8, EI 100 → 15.6,
   EI 400 → 98.0, EI 3000 → 233.1. Inverted, the ISO 3000 film needs fifteen times *more* light than
   the ISO 100 film. This is asserted in the audit, not argued.
4. The two already-adopted sets are stored **unnegated**, and this reader reproduces them from the
   vector paths to **rms 0.034 and 0.027 decades**. A mirror does not agree with its original to a
   thirtieth of a decade.

⚠ **Following this row would have mirrored two correct sets in the course of "fixing" them.**

**Adopted:** `POLAROID_52` and `POLAROID_55_PN_NEG` — the two new pan sets the row promised. 664 and
667 confirmed and left alone.

⚠ **The row's second claim was also wrong.** It said *"667 and 55 … the label sweep returns one or
two labels each — they need their own windows"*. They need none. All four panels are found by one
rule that keys on the **axis labels** rather than a page region, and 667 returns four y labels at a
dead-uniform 40.3 pt per decade. A fixed window was the blocker, not the layout.

**Also corrected:** the criterion string on all four, from `log_energy_for_neutral_density_0.75` —
which names the measurement and contradicts what is stored — to
`log_reciprocal_erg_cm2_neutral_D0.75`.

⚠ **And the "0" tick is not a decade.** Three of the four y axes print a tick labelled `0` one full
decade below `1`. On a log axis that position is 0.1 and zero has no place on it. Only 1 / 10 / 100 /
1000 are fitted.
