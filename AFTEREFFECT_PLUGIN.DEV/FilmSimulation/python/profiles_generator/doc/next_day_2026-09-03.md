## 1. The three things worth doing first, in order

### 1.1 C45 — the clump census now has three independent sources and no decision

This is the largest open fidelity question in the database and it is **no longer a
one-source claim**:

| source | measures | implies grain |
|---|---|---|
| BBC Report T-101 Table 2 | equivalent grain diameter, six emulsions, printed | **0.59–1.43 µm** |
| Takano 1969 Fig. 13 + Fig. 8 | optical autocorrelation and Selwyn aperture series | 0.87–4.64 µm, median 2.46 |
| **Takano 1968 Fig. 11 (new today)** | Expected mottle 3.98–6.81 µm over the paper's own stated 5–8× aggregate factor | **0.50–1.36 µm** |

**The 175 stored `clump_um_g` values have median 13.0**, outside all three by an order of
magnitude. C45 was refused on measured render cost (rendered grain rms goes 13.9/17.8/18.2 at
clump 13.0 against 15.4/24.2/29.3 at clump 2.46, across 960/2000/4000 px). ⚠ **What has
changed is the evidence, not the cost** — the correction now has agreement from three
laboratories in three countries across three decades. It moves a pixel on 168 stocks, so it
stays the owner's call, and it is the first thing to put to him.

### 1.2 The [VTD] / [VE] route dependence — a measured effect with no parameter

Takano 1968 measures that **reaching a density by developing longer gives a 36–40 % smaller
clump than reaching it by exposing more**. The engine has push/pull processing and renders one
grain for both routes. This is a schema question (one scalar on `GrainSpec`, driven by
`ProcessVariant.push_stops`), it is cheap, and it is inert at its default — but it is a new
field and therefore the owner's decision. Knowledge base §23n.

### 1.3 Spectral sensitivity for the three new Fuji stocks

`FUJI_PROVIA_100F`, `FUJICOLOR_SUPERIA_XTRA_400` and `FUJICOLOR_PRO_400H` shipped with
`spectral.has_data` False. The panels are located and their geometry is recorded in
`fuji_t3_2026.py`; the blocker is that two of the three scale that ordinate with a bracketed
arrow marked "1.0" rather than a numbered ladder. **What would settle it in one step**: any
Fuji publication printing those curves against a numbered sensitivity axis — the PROVIA 400X
bulletin AF3-0213E does exactly that for its own film, so the layout exists.

## 2. What the owner could search for, if he wants a target

Three, specific, and each closes something already framed:

1. **A Fuji sheet with a NUMBERED spectral-sensitivity ordinate for PROVIA 100F, SUPERIA
   X-TRA 400 or PRO 400H.** Closes §1.3 above outright. Search on the AF3 code plus a
   different revision letter, or the Japanese-market 「データシート」 editions.
2. **Takano 1968 Part 1** — «写真像の粒状性(第1報)», J. Soc. Phot. Sci. Japan, 1967 or 1968.
   Part 2 (read today) refers to it for the DEFINITION of Expected mottle size and for the
   5–8× aggregate factor, both of which this project is now leaning on and neither of which
   is derived in Part 2. It would turn a quoted factor into a derived one.
3. **Any MTF panel for KODAK GOLD 200.** E-7022 has none in any of its editions; the film is
   in the database with an estimated f50. A Kodak Alaris consumer-film comparison chart or an
   independent laboratory measurement would do.
